How I approach pursuit and proposal storytelling

Persuasive deliverables follow the same discipline as my enterprise programmes: frame the problem, shape the story, produce with craft, then review hard.

  1. Frame the buyer’s problem

    Run workshops and stakeholder sessions with business leaders to define what the audience must believe and decide.

  2. Shape the story

    Turn complex requirements into a clear narrative, with the design rationale to defend every choice.

  3. Design and produce

    Deliver presentations, visual systems, video, motion and digital assets, accelerated with AI-assisted tools.

  4. Review for quality

    Hold design-quality reviews for accuracy, brand compliance and consistency before anything ships.

Proposal and pursuit storytelling is a transferable strength, built through executive presentations, workshops and stakeholder reviews on enterprise programmes.
